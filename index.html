⚡ Symmetric vs. Asymmetric Vector Kernels

On Zen 4 / AVX-512, symmetric INT4 quantization outpaced asymmetric INT4 by 4%–10% in generation throughput across every group size. Symmetric dequantization eliminates runtime zero-point subtraction, significantly lowering register pressure.

Throughput: 45.93 tok/s (Symmetric) vs. 44.01 tok/s (Asymmetric) at g=128

🧩 Hidden Dimension Divisibility ($d_{\text{model}} = 896$)

Qwen2.5-0.5B's hidden dimension (896) is evenly divisible by 32 (28), 64 (14), and 128 (7), but fails on 256 ($896 / 256 = 3.5$). Self-attention projections required fallback to $g=128$, while MLP projections ($d_{\text{ffn}} = 4864$) scaled directly to $g=256$.

Resolved via OpenVINO's GroupSizeFallbackMode.ADJUST

🎯 AWQ Salient Channel Weight Protection

Standard PTQ minimizes tensor MSE uniformly. AWQ observes activation magnitudes on the 64-sample clinical calibration corpus, protecting the top 0.5% salient channels from clipping error and achieving the highest generation throughput (47.10 tok/s).

Fastest throughput with 0.015 repetition ratio

🧠 Dynamic Dequantization (WNA16) vs W4A4

Weights are compressed to 4-bit in memory and dequantized on-the-fly to FP16 in registers. Activations remain in FP16, capturing 70% bandwidth savings while eliminating activation outlier quantization collapse.

3.06x memory reduction with zero clinical quality loss
